Beware of the curse !

"The essence of the night is but a dire omen of fear."


Curse is a board game where two players face off using cards with unique effects in order to avoid ending up with the curse by their side.

Each ghost has a unique effect ; passing the curse, adding or removing a card from the hand, canceling or copying a card effect.
Each player starts with six cards in hand, and one of the two players has the curse. On each turn, each player plays a single card from their hand in order to pass the curse to the other player or to gain a useful advantage later.

Curse is available for free in printable and digital versions on the Steam Workshop of Tabletop Simulator.
