Beware of the Curse !

"The very essence of night is but a dire omen of fear."


Curse is a board game where two players face off using cards with unique effects in order to avoid ending up with the curse by their side.

Each ghost has a different effect; pass the curse, add or remove a card from your hand, cancel or copy a card effect.
Each player starts with six cards in hand, and one of the two players has the curse. Turn by turn, each player plays a single card from their hand in order to pass the curse to the other player or gain a useful advantage subsequently.

Curse is available for free in printable version and digital version on the Steam Workshop of Tabletop Simulator.
